Use Cases

Simple protection for real-life moments

Every situation uses the same quiet building blocks — choose only what fits your day.

Walk-home timer

Walking Home Safe

Start a 15, 30, or 60-minute walk-home timer in one tap. Tap Done when you're safe — if you don't, your angels get an alarm with your location, even if your phone is taken or turned off.

Immediate duress

Hidden Emergency Gesture

In immediate danger on the street or at a party? A discreet triple-tap on the back of your iPhone (Back Tap), Action Button, or holding the quiet Sync button alerts every angel immediately.

Forced access

Forced Phone Unlock

Forced to unlock your phone or open a banking app? An iOS automation silently starts a short countdown. Only a subtle double-tap cancels it — otherwise your angels are alerted automatically.

Desktop check-in

Coerced Laptop Sign-In

Every time your Windows PC unlocks, a silent background agent starts a short timer. Press your secret hotkey to check in; if someone forces access, watchers in other homes raise the alarm.

How It Works

Resilient by design — zero central servers

Your devices and your friends' always-on watchers meet at public encrypted relays. As long as one watcher is running, your alarm gets out.

1

Trigger

Started from your phone (Sync timer, Back Tap, automation) or Windows PC unlock.

2

Encrypted Relays

Messages travel wrapped through multiple public Nostr relays so no single host can block them.

3

Independent Watchers

Always-on Docker or PC watchers in friends' homes keep the clocks and escalate only if needed.

4

Your Angels

Trusted contacts receive Web Push and Telegram alerts with one-tap I am calling and Resolved actions.

Download & Install

Get protected — or help protect someone

Install the discreet mobile web app on your phone, protect your Windows laptop, or run a watcher for your circle.

Phone Web App ("Sync")

iPhone (iOS 16.4+) & Android

Deliberately named Sync on your home screen. Works both as your safety app (timer, silent button, invite links) and to receive push alarms as someone's angel.

On iPhone (Safari) 1. Open the app in Safari and tap Share.
2. Tap Add to Home Screen and launch Sync from there.
On Android (Chrome) 1. Open the app in Chrome.
2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home Screen).
Open & Install Web App

Windows Agent

warn-agent.exe · Silent PC agent

Runs invisibly in the background on Windows. Detects every logon and unlock, checks your secret hotkey, supports a duress key, and optionally runs a built-in watcher.

.\warn-agent.exe init --device laptop
.\warn-agent.exe run
Download for Windows

Docker Watcher

warn-watcher · Home server / NAS / Mini-PC

Run an always-on watcher in another household. Makes outbound connections only — no open ports, reverse proxies, or port forwarding needed.

services:
  watcher:
    build: .
    restart: unless-stopped
    volumes:
      - ./data:/data

# Start with:
docker compose up -d --build

Privacy & Trust

Built for safety without surveillance

Unlike family-locator apps, Angel never turns safety into constant tracking.

No central server

Every message goes through replaceable relays to your own circle's watchers — no company can shut it down.

Not a location tracker

Nobody can follow where you are. Your position is included only when an alarm actually fires.

End-to-end encrypted

Nostr NIP-59 gift wraps and RFC 8291 Web Push keep relays, Apple, and Google blind to your alerts.

Discreet by design

Plain Sync icon on your phone and a silent Windows agent with no popups or default hotkeys.

Angel — Open-source decentralized duress & check-in network.

Complements and never replaces official emergency services. Always call your local emergency number first when it is safe to do so.
