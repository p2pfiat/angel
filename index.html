Use Cases

Simple protection for real-life moments

Every situation uses the same quiet building blocks — choose only what fits your day.

Walk-home timer

Walking Home Safe

Start a 15, 30, or 60-minute walk-home timer in one tap. Tap Done when you're safe — if you don't, your angels get an alarm with your location, even if your phone is taken or turned off.

Immediate duress

Hidden Emergency Gesture

In immediate danger on iPhone or Android? Holding the quiet Sync button sends an immediate encrypted alert with your location — and on iPhone you can also trigger it with a secret gesture or shortcut of your choice.

Forced access

Forced Phone Unlock

Forced to unlock your phone? You can configure Sync on iPhone or Android to send automatically when opened, and on iPhone combine it with Shortcuts when opening a banking app — cancelled only by your own secret check-in gesture.

Desktop check-in

Coerced Laptop Sign-In

Every time your Windows PC unlocks, a silent background agent starts a short timer. Press your secret hotkey to check in; if someone forces access, watchers in other homes raise the alarm.

Discreetly named Sync on your home screen. Works on both iPhone and Android to start walk-home timers, send silent alarms, set iPhone Shortcuts, and receive instant alerts as someone's angel.

Windows Agent & Watcher

warn-agent.exe · Silent PC protection + optional watcher

Runs invisibly in the background on Windows. Detects every logon and unlock, checks your secret hotkey, and can run a built-in watcher ([watcher]) for yourself or to back up the starter server.

.\warn-agent.exe init --device laptop
.\warn-agent.exe run

Docker Watcher

warn-watcher · Home server / NAS / Mini-PC

Run an always-on watcher in another household. Makes outbound connections only — no open ports, reverse proxies, or port forwarding needed.

services:
  watcher:
    build: .
    restart: unless-stopped
    volumes:
      - ./data:/data

# Start with:
docker compose up -d --build

Privacy & Trust

Built for safety without surveillance

Unlike family-locator apps, Angel never turns safety into constant tracking.

No central server

Every message goes through replaceable relays to your own circle's watchers — no company can shut it down.

Not a location tracker

Nobody can follow where you are. Your position is included only when an alarm actually fires.

End-to-end encrypted

Nostr NIP-59 gift wraps and RFC 8291 Web Push keep relays, Apple, and Google blind to your alerts.

Discreet by design

Plain Sync icon on your phone and a silent Windows agent with no popups or default hotkeys.

FAQ

Common questions

Everything you and your angels need to know before getting started.

Can my angels track my location day to day?

No. Sync never streams background GPS or keeps a location history. Your coordinates are attached only at the moment you trigger an alarm or start a walk-home timer — and if you check in safely, your angels hear nothing at all.

Do I need my own server to get started?

No. You can start free in 60 seconds using our built-in starter server right inside Sync. Whenever you're ready for full independence, you can add your own Windows or Docker watcher in another home — Sync talks to all of them at once.

What happens if my phone is taken or switched off during a timer?

Your check-in clock runs on the watchers, not on your phone. Once you start a walk-home timer or open-app check-in, an attacker turning off or smashing your phone cannot stop the alarm from going out when the clock runs out.

What if I trigger an alarm or miss a check-in by accident?

iPhone Shortcut alarms wait through a 20-second cancel window so running your OK shortcut stops them silently. Even if a timer alarm has already fired, tapping OK sends a calm update to your angels and you can use Send test alarm anytime to rehearse safely.

Is Angel free — and does it replace emergency services?

Yes, Angel and Sync are free and open source with no account or subscription required. Angel complements — and never replaces — official emergency services (like 112 or 911); always call emergency services directly whenever it is safe to do so.
