On an iPhone, alarms arrive only in Sync on the Home Screen:

  1. Tap Copy invite.
  2. No Sync on your Home Screen yet? Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open Sync from the Home Screen. If it does not show this invite by itself, go to Settings and tap Paste invite.
